IELTS Grammar

Adjectives

Comparatives, superlatives, opposites, emphasis and adjective order.

Comparative & superlative

Use the comparative for two things. Use the superlative for more than two things.

Adjective Comparative Superlative
short adjectives -er than -est
good better than the best
bad worse than the worst
long adjectives more than the most

Opposites with un-

Add un- to make the opposite of these adjectives.

happy→unhappy
intelligent→unintelligent
kind→unkind
friendly→unfriendly
comfortable→uncomfortable

So & such

So and such give extra emphasis to an idea.

She is so kind.so + adjective
She is such a kind person.such + (adjective) + noun

Too & enough

Use too before an adjective. Use enough after an adjective.

I cannot drink this coffee. It is too hot.too + adjective
Now it is cool enough to drink.adjective + enough
